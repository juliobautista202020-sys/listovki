Privacy policy

Effective 25.10.2026. Applies to the “Listovki: Yasno” mobile app for Android and iOS and to this website. The Bulgarian version prevails.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is ASOINCOL – Asociación Colombo-Lanzaroteña por la Solidaridad y la Integración Sociocultural en la Isla (OpeTest Apps), Calle Alcalde Lorenzo Cabrera 23, 35500 Arrecife, Spain. For any privacy question write to hola@opetest.es.

2. In short

  • The app works without sign-up or an account. We never ask for your name, email or phone.
  • Your progress (answers, mock exams, plan, favourites, settings) stays on your device only. We have no server for user data and never receive it.
  • We use no analytics, tracking, profiling or ads.
  • Data reaches third parties only in the cases in section 4: purchase, video downloads and emails to support.

5. Legal basis (Art. 6 GDPR)

  • Contract – buying and restoring Pro and downloading the video packs you request.
  • Legitimate interest – answering support requests and reports.
  • Legal obligation – accounting and tax rules the stores apply to sales.

6. How long we keep data

Data on your device stays until you delete it or the app. Support emails are kept for up to 12 months after the request is closed. Retention for purchases is set by Google and Apple.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection. Because we do not store your progress on a server, most rights are exercised directly in the app (delete, export). For anything else write to hola@opetest.es; we reply within one month.

You may lodge a complaint with the Commission for Personal Data Protection, 2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd., Sofia 1592, Bulgaria, www.cpdp.bg.

8. Children

The app is meant for learner drivers and is not directed at children under 14. We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

9. Security

Data on the device is protected by the operating system. Connections for purchases and downloads are encrypted (HTTPS). Every downloaded video is integrity-checked before use.

10. Changes

If this policy changes we will publish the new version on this page with a new date, and notify you in the app of significant changes.
